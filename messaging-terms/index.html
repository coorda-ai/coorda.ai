Messaging Terms

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

1. Overview

Coorda Corp. ("Coorda," "we," "us," or "our") uses messaging channels including SMS, WhatsApp, and RCS to communicate with users of our construction coordination platform. These Messaging Terms explain how we collect consent, what messages you will receive, and how to opt out.

2. Consent and Opt-In

By providing your phone number to Coorda, either directly or through your crew manager or employer, you may receive an opt-in confirmation message. You must reply YES to confirm your consent before receiving project-related messages.

You are not required to opt in to messaging as a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

3. Types of Messages

Messages from Coorda may include:

  • Project schedule updates and changes
  • Task assignments and coordination
  • Crew notifications and reminders
  • Account and service notifications
  • Responses to your questions or requests

4. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on project activity and your interaction with the platform. During active projects, you may receive multiple messages per day.

5. Opt-Out

You can opt out of messaging at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive a confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. If you wish to re-enroll, reply YES or contact us at inquiries@coorda.ai.

6. Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at inquiries@coorda.ai.

7. Costs

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Coorda does not charge for messages, but your carrier may.

8. Privacy

Your phone number and messaging data are handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not sell your phone number or messaging data to third parties.

9. Supported Channels

These terms apply to all messaging channels used by Coorda, including but not limited to SMS, WhatsApp, and RCS.

10. Changes

We may update these Messaging Terms from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
