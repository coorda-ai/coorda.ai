How Coorda Messaging Works

When a coordinator adds a team member's phone number to Coorda, we send an opt-in confirmation message before any project messages are delivered. Workers must confirm consent before receiving messages. Here's how it works.

Step 1: Coordinator Obtains Verbal Consent

Before entering a worker's information into Coorda, the coordinator obtains verbal authorization from the worker. This typically happens on-site or during crew onboarding.

"I'd like to add your number to Coorda so you'll get schedule updates, task assignments, and crew notifications on your phone. Do I have your permission to sign you up?"

Once the worker agrees, the coordinator proceeds to enter their information into Coorda, which triggers the SMS opt-in confirmation.

Step 2: Coordinator Adds a Team Member

A crew coordinator enters a worker's name and phone number into Coorda when assigning them to a project. Coorda does not send any project messages until the worker confirms consent.

COORDA
Carlos Rivera
+1 (555) 234-5678
English

An opt-in message will be sent to this number.

Step 3: Worker Receives Opt-In Message

The worker receives a branded message from Coorda asking them to confirm they want to receive project messages.

SMS
Coorda
COORDA
Hi Carlos, your coordinator Maria Lopez at Apex Electric has added you to a project on Coorda. You'll receive schedule updates, task assignments, and crew notifications for your job sites. Reply YES to confirm. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Msg & data rates may apply. coorda.ai/messaging-terms

Step 4: Worker Confirms

The worker replies YES to confirm they want to receive messages. They receive a confirmation.

SMS
Coorda
COORDA
Hi Carlos, your coordinator Maria Lopez at Apex Electric has added you to a project on Coorda. Reply YES to confirm. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
YES
COORDA
You're confirmed! You'll now receive project updates from Coorda. Reply HELP for assistance or STOP to opt out at any time.

Step 5: Worker Receives Project Messages

Once confirmed, the worker receives relevant project messages — schedule updates, task assignments, and crew notifications.

Opting Out

Workers can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. They will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

SMS
Coorda
STOP
COORDA
You've been unsubscribed from Coorda messages. You will not receive any further messages. Reply YES to re-subscribe, or contact inquiries@coorda.ai.
  • Message frequency varies by project activity. Workers may receive multiple messages per day during active projects.
  • Coorda does not charge for messages. Standard carrier message and data rates may apply.
  • Phone numbers and messaging data are handled per our Privacy Policy. We do not sell personal data.
  • For help, reply HELP to any message or email inquiries@coorda.ai.
