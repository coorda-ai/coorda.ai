What is Coorda?

Coorda is a coordination platform for construction teams. We help subcontractors and general contractors manage schedules, crews, and site communication — through the messaging channels workers already use, like SMS, WhatsApp, and RCS.

Our AI assistant helps coordinators plan their week, track crew assignments, and stay ahead of schedule changes across multiple job sites. Workers receive updates in their preferred language, on their existing phone — no app to install, no training required.

Coorda is currently in private beta with select construction teams in the United States.

How It Works

1

Plan with AI

Your AI assistant helps you build and adjust weekly schedules across all your job sites. Assign crews, manage manpower, and spot conflicts before they hit the field.

2

Reach every worker

Schedule updates, task assignments, and notifications are sent through SMS, WhatsApp, or RCS — in each worker's preferred language. Nothing to download.

3

Stay in sync

Weather delays, crew changes, schedule shifts — Coorda keeps everyone informed automatically so you can focus on running your sites.

Who It's For

Coorda is built for subcontractor companies and general contractors managing field crews across multiple construction sites — electrical, plumbing, HVAC, concrete, and other trades. If you're spending your mornings on phone calls figuring out who's going where, Coorda is for you.
